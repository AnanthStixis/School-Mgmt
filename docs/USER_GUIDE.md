# User guide – BNM Public School fees and records

Short how-tos for each role, used for training in the week before go-live.
Open the system at the address the school gives you (for example `https://fees.bnmpschool.in`).

## Signing in

| Who | Tab | How |
|---|---|---|
| Staff with a password | **Staff** | E-mail and password |
| Staff without a password | **Staff** → *No password? Sign in with a code…* | Your mobile number, then the 6-digit code sent by SMS |
| Parents | **Parent** | The mobile number registered with the school, then the code |
| Students | **Student** | Admission number and the password a parent set |

You stay signed in for up to 30 days on your own device. Use **Sign out** on shared computers.

## Accounts office

### Every day
1. **Collect at the counter** – Fees → **Collect**. Search the student (name, admission number or parent's mobile).
   - Tick the installments being paid. Change the amount for a part payment.
   - Late fee (₹25 a day after the due date): **Collect**, **Waive** (give a reason), or **Later**. When clearing an installment it must be collected or waived.
   - Choose the mode. Cheque and DD need bank, number and date (no post-dated cheques).
   - **Collect** → *Open & print* for the receipt. The parent also gets an SMS.
2. **Online payments** – Fees → **Online payments**: every attempt from parents. Only "Paid" ones have a receipt. "Paid twice" amounts need a refund.
3. **End of day** – Fees → **Receipts** for today: totals by mode should match the cash and cheques in hand.
4. **Cheques and net banking** – when the bank statement shows the money, open the receipt → **Money received**. A bounced cheque → **Bounced** (the receipt is cancelled and the balance comes back).

### When something is wrong
- **Wrong receipt** → open it → **Cancel receipt** with a reason, then collect again. Only the newest receipt on an installment can be cancelled.
- **Concession** (sibling, mid-year joining…) → student profile → fee → **Concession**: an amount or %, spread equally, in order, or per installment. It replaces any earlier concession.
- **Student leaving** → refund what was paid (receipt → **Refund…**) and **Write off** what is still unpaid (student profile → fee → Write off).
- **Late fee to forgive** without a payment → student profile → **Waive** next to the late fee.

### Every month
- Fees → **Reports → Dues** → **Send SMS reminders** (each parent gets at most one a day).
- **Class summary** and **Concessions & refunds** for the management meeting; each downloads as CSV.

### Start of the year
- Fees → **Fee setup**: check the class bands and amounts; tick *Amounts confirmed*.
- Fees → **Assign & publish**, class by class: **Assign → Confirm → Publish to parents → Online on**.

## Branch manager / School Admin

- **Students** – add or edit students and guardians; **Import CSV** for many at once (a SchoolElement export works as it is).
- **Staff** – add staff and give them a login (teacher, accounts; manager only by the School Admin).
- **Academic Setup** – classes, sections, class teachers, subjects.
- **Admissions** – enquiries from first contact to enrolment; enrolling assigns the school fee automatically.
- **Attendance** – see trends and the low-attendance list; you can correct any day.
- Everything the accounts office can do in Fees, plus setting a student's sign-in (student profile → *Student sign-in*).
- The School Admin also manages **Branches & Config** (timings, working days, late fee per day).

## Class teacher

- **Attendance** each morning: pick your class, mark Absent or Leave (everyone starts as Present), **Submit**. Parents of absent children get an SMS. You can correct the last 2 days.

## Parents

- **Fees** – what is due, paid and overdue for each child. **Pay online** pays whole installments by UPI, card or net banking; the receipt comes by SMS and is under *Receipts*.
- **Attendance** – a calendar for each child.
- **Your children's sign-in** (on the home page) – set or change a password so your child can sign in on the Student tab with their admission number; turn it off any time.

## Who to call

Problems signing in or with a payment: the school's accounts office. They can see every online payment attempt and its bank reference.
